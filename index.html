Privacy Policy

Orb Tanker · Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short: Orb Tanker has no accounts, no servers and no analytics of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service needs in order to show you an advert, and we have configured it to send as little as possible.

1. Summary

Orb Tanker works mostly offline. A small Google AdMob banner appears at the bottom of the screen to support the free version. Players who confirm they are 13 or older may also choose to watch an optional video advert after failing a level, in exchange for 5 bonus fire rounds for that level.

We do not operate accounts, do not have our own servers, and do not run our own analytics or tracking.

2. Advertising, and the age question

On first launch the game asks whether you are under 13. This exists so that advertising can be appropriate for your age. Your answer is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted to us or to Google.

You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings screen.

Your answerBanner advertOptional video advert
Under 13 Shown, but non-personalised Not offered
13 or older Shown, standard personalisation may apply Offered after a failed level
Not answered yet No advertising is requested at all

AdMob is Google's advertising SDK. Google may receive this device's advertising identifier in order to personalise advertising for players confirmed to be 13 or older. For players confirmed to be under 13, the advert request is flagged as child-directed, which prevents that identifier from being transmitted at all. Advertising for under-13 players is non-personalised: there is no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.

3. European consent and personalised advertising

Orb Tanker does not ask for consent for personalised advertising and never displays a consent form.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, advertising there is limited to non-personalised ads for every player, whatever their age. If you are elsewhere, players confirmed to be 13 or older may still receive personalised advertising.

The game contacts Google's consent service on each launch to check the consent status for your region, but it never puts a question to you and records no answer.

4. What is stored on your device

The following is kept in your device's local storage only:

  • High scores and star ratings
  • Level progress
  • Sound and music settings
  • Your answer to the advertising age question
  • Any bonus fire rounds you have earned but not yet used

None of this is uploaded, shared or sold. Uninstalling the app erases all of it.

5. What network requests the app makes

The game loads its own bundled images and sounds, and makes no network requests of its own. All network activity comes from two places:

  • Google AdMob, serving the banner and video adverts
  • Google's consent service, contacted on each launch to check whether a consent form is required in your region

For details on how Google AdMob collects, uses and shares data, see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Advertising identifier

Your device's advertising identifier is a resettable identifier assigned by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete it from your device's Android Settings, and you can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

7. Children

Orb Tanker is intended for a general audience and is suitable for players of all ages. Where a player confirms they are under 13, we apply the stricter advertising settings described in section 2: non-personalised advertising only, no video advert, and no transmission of the device's advertising identifier.

8. Your rights

Because your progress and settings never leave your device, we hold no personal data about you and there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. To erase everything the app has stored, uninstall the app. To withdraw your advertising age answer, reopen Settings and change it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the game's release notes.
